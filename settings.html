Normal-mode poll interval Saved ✓

seconds between checks

How often to poll the Available tab while idle (no recent orders). Lower = alarm sooner on the first order of a bulk, but more requests/day. When a new order appears we switch to Fast mode (~0.5s, uncapped) for 3 min to catch the rest of the bulk, then fall back here. Default 20, minimum 5.

Quote rate table Saved ✓

City Zip Exterior $ Interior $

One row per City + Zip. When a new order's city AND zip both match (case-insensitive), we auto-quote at the Exterior or Interior rate depending on the order type. No matching row, or a blank rate for that type → the order is skipped (alarm only, never quoted). Due Date = current due +48h, Quote Expires +24h, Reason "Standard rate". No rows = quoting disabled (pure alarm).

Daily quote limit Saved ✓

Stop after this many quotes submitted in a day (local time) — unpriced/skipped and failed do not count. When the limit is hit, the monitored tab is closed and polling stops — the session goes idle. 0 or blank = no limit. Resets at local midnight.
